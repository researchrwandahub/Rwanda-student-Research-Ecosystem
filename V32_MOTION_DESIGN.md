# V32 — RSRE Motion Design

Added a restrained motion system for the frontend:

- page entrance fade/slide
- staggered card reveals on key dashboards
- subtle lift/shadow interaction for research cards
- button press feedback
- animated progress sweep and width transition
- live-dot pulse
- loading shimmer utility
- reduced-motion accessibility support

Design rule: motion communicates hierarchy and state; it does not distract from academic/research content.
