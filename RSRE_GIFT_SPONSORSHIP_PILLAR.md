# RSRE Gift Sponsorship

Students are not asked to pay in the core RSRE/RSJH learning and research workflow. A separate sponsor-facing gift flow allows supporters to fund a student/researcher.

Flow:
1. Supporter clicks Give a Gift.
2. Supporter provides recipient email and selects purpose/payment method.
3. RSRE records a pending sponsorship.
4. Payment is confirmed through the configured payment process (gateway or administrator).
5. RSRE generates a gift code and emails it to the recipient.
6. Recipient signs in and redeems the gift code.

The journal editorial workflow is untouched. Gift funding must never be used as a publication acceptance/reviewer decision shortcut.
