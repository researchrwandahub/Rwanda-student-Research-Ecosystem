# Research Academy V3 – Full Learning Engine

This build focuses only on Research Academy until its workflow is complete.

## Completion rule
Learners progress through required lessons, quizzes, practical labs and research assignments. Required assessments must pass before the next module/level unlocks.

## Core capabilities
- course enrollment and cohort participation
- module/lesson progression
- quizzes and multiple attempts
- practical research labs
- graded assignments with rubrics
- prerequisites and unlocking
- badges and certificate triggers
- learning records/transcript events
- course announcements
- email + WhatsApp + in-app notifications through RSRE shared services
- admin course/content/assessment endpoints
