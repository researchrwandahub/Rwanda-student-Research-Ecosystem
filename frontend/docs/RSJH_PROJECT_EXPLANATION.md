# RSJH in one minute

RSJH is a student-centred research and publishing ecosystem. It helps health students discover opportunities, develop ideas, submit manuscripts, experience real peer review, respond to feedback, publish and build a visible scholarly record.

**The difference:** the journal is the publication layer; the platform is the research-development layer around it.

**Journey:** Idea → Develop → Opportunities → Submit → Editorial screening → Peer review → Revise → Decision → Publish → Research record.

**Governance:** reviewers recommend, editors decide, and the Editor-in-Chief retains final publication authority. MedTech Rwanda provides technology and publisher-side infrastructure while scientific decisions remain with RSJH editorial leadership.

**30-second pitch:** RSJH is Rwanda's student-centred research and publishing ecosystem. We help health students move from curiosity to research, from research to peer review, and from peer review to publication while building a lifelong research identity.
