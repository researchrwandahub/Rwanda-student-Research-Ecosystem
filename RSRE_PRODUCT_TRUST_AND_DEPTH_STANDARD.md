# RSRE Product Trust & Depth Standard

## Product identity
RSRE is the Research Support and Research Ecosystem. RSJH is the Rwanda Student Journal for Health. MedTech AI is a supporting research-assistance capability. These names must not be used interchangeably.

## Research tools
Every research tool must have a clear job, a real backend state model, meaningful empty/loading/error states and a visible distinction between evidence, user declaration, metadata and inference.

## Academy
A lesson is not considered substantive because it reaches an arbitrary character count. Content quality requires explanation, examples, practical work, assessment and references appropriate to the learning objective.

## Public Passport
Public visibility is opt-in. Researchers select which profile fields can be displayed publicly. Evidence, private notes and unpublished work remain private unless the product explicitly provides a separate approved sharing mechanism.

## Opportunities
RSRE may provide both internally curated/synchronized opportunities and a clearly labelled list of external discovery sources. External listings are not endorsements. Users must verify deadlines, eligibility and application routes on official organiser sites.

## Content integrity
Never invent statistics, partnerships, accreditation, endorsements, publication claims, testimonials, funding, credentials or institutional relationships. When information is unavailable, say so.

## UX quality
Avoid repetitive AI-generated marketing language, decorative complexity without purpose, excessive gradients, fake metrics and template-like copy. Prefer clear language, useful hierarchy and interfaces that show the actual state of the system.
