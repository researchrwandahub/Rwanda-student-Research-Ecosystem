# RSRE Research Academy — Learning Product Standard

## Purpose

The Academy is a serious research-learning product inside RSRE. It is not a content wall with a completion button.

The benchmark is the quality of the learning experience, not a fixed word count or a timer. IBM SkillsBuild publicly describes courses in the 60–90 minute range, offers learning pathways, progress tracking and digital credentials, and uses assessments and project-based practical learning for some programmes. RSRE should learn from those product principles without copying IBM's brand, interface, copy or information architecture.

## RSRE lesson standard

A core lesson should normally contain enough material to support a real learning session. The current engineering baseline is:

- 1,200 characters minimum for an active core lesson.
- 800 characters minimum for an active practical activity lesson.
- 1,600+ characters is the preferred starting point for a substantive text lesson, with room for deeper treatment when the topic requires it.

Character count is a quality gate, not the definition of a good lesson. A lesson is strong when the learner can understand the idea, see how it works, apply it, and check their understanding.

## Recommended lesson anatomy

Depending on the topic, a lesson should use a useful combination of:

1. Why the topic matters
2. Core explanation
3. Key terms or concepts
4. Worked example
5. Rwanda/Africa-relevant example where appropriate
6. Practical task or case
7. Common mistake or misconception
8. Check your understanding
9. Short reflection or next step
10. Further reading / resource

Not every lesson needs every component. The author should choose the components that actually improve learning.

## Module anatomy

A substantial module should normally provide:

- clear learning objectives
- a visible lesson sequence
- realistic estimated learning time
- supporting resources
- practical application where appropriate
- a knowledge check or assessment
- transparent pass requirements
- persistent progress
- a clear relationship between learning and credentials

## Credential discipline

RSRE must distinguish learning completion from credentials. A learner should not earn a credential merely by clicking a button. Where an assessment is required, the backend must enforce the stated pass threshold and the required learning activities must be completed.

## Writing style

RSRE course content should sound like a competent research educator wrote it for learners.

Prefer:

- specific explanations
- concrete research examples
- direct sentences
- active voice
- careful claims
- plain academic English
- relevant Rwanda/Africa context
- references that learners can actually consult

Avoid:

- generic AI marketing language
- repetitive introductions
- empty motivational phrases
- fake statistics
- exaggerated promises
- unnecessary headings
- padding content simply to increase character count
- invented institutional authority

## Content honesty

Where a lesson is not yet developed enough, the Academy administration should flag it for development. Do not make the learner wait on an artificial timer to disguise shallow content.

## Product principles borrowed from mature learning platforms

RSRE can use the following general principles as design references:

- show learners what they are learning and why
- make progression visible
- use meaningful assessments
- provide practical work, not only reading
- treat credentials as evidence of achievement
- let experienced learners enter at an appropriate level
- keep learning connected to real-world goals

These are product principles, not a request to reproduce another organization's visual design.


## 2026-09-09 research-tool hardening
- Research Discovery must expose source availability and distinguish unknown metadata from negative findings. External scholarly sources remain metadata providers; users verify the original record before citing.
- Research Passport may be public only when the researcher explicitly chooses public visibility and selects fields. Private evidence is never exposed by the public endpoint. Public Passport is an identity/portfolio surface, not a professional licence, degree, ethics approval, or quality guarantee.
- Research Opportunities includes a curated external-source shelf for young researchers. RSRE does not endorse or guarantee third-party listings; every application must be verified against the organiser's official website.
- The Academy standard is based on substantive learning experiences, practical work, assessment and credible progression rather than a simple character threshold.
