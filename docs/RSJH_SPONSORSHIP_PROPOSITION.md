# RSJH Sponsorship Proposition

Support RSJH as research-capacity infrastructure for the next generation of health professionals, not simply as a website.

## Partnership packages
- Student Research Access Partner — hosting, access and onboarding.
- Peer Review Partner — reviewer training and recognition.
- Research Opportunity Partner — discovery and matching.
- Responsible AI Partner — capped, transparent research-learning assistance.
- RSJH Student Research Challenge Partner — prizes, mentorship and an annual challenge.

## What sponsors receive
Appropriate visibility, measurable reach and impact reporting, participation in a responsible research initiative, and opportunities to support workshops or student challenges without controlling editorial decisions.

## What RSJH protects
Scientific editorial independence, peer-review integrity, transparency, and merit-based publication.
