# University Rankings

Goal

Provide a lightweight ranking of universities by research output in RSJH.

Ranking factors

- Number of publications
- Average citations (if available)
- Downloads and views
- Collaboration index (co-authorship across institutions)

Output

- Table with sortable columns
- Map showing university locations and counts
- Exportable CSV for the rankings

Notes

This is a relative ranking for RSJH publications and should be used for internal benchmarking rather than formal academic ranking.
