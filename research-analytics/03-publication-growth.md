# Publication Growth

Purpose

Measure RSJH publication growth over time and by institution.

Metrics

- Total publications per year
- New author rate (first-time authors per year)
- Repeat author rate
- Publications per university

Visuals

- Bar chart of publications per year
- Stacked area chart by university or category
- KPI cards for year-over-year growth (%)

Notes

Start with the local article store; when backend is available, query the API for aggregated counts.
