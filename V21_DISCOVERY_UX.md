# RSRE V21 — Research Discovery UX

Research Discovery is now positioned as an evidence-navigation experience rather than a generic search page.

## UX additions
- Save-search affordance for continuity.
- Evidence summary cards: total records, local RSJH signal, next move.
- Research pathway: Discover -> Compare -> Find a gap -> Build.
- Existing RSJH, OpenAlex and Crossref search behavior is preserved.
- Source-status transparency remains visible.

## Architectural boundary
Discovery finds and contextualizes evidence. It does not replace Analytics, Sandbox, Incubator, or the RSJH editorial workflow.
