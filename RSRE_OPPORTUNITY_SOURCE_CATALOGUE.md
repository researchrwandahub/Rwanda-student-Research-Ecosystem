# RSRE Opportunity Source Catalogue

These are external discovery sources provided for young researchers and students. They are not RSRE-owned services and listing them does not mean RSRE endorses a specific opportunity, organiser, eligibility decision or outcome. Users should verify every deadline, eligibility rule, fee and application route on the official organiser website.
