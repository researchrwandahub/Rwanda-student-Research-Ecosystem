# Public Health Dashboard (Read this doc)

Purpose

The Public Health Dashboard is a companion analytics project for RSJH that aggregates published research into actionable public health insights for Rwanda.

What the dashboard provides

- Top Diseases Published — which disease areas attract the most student research.
- Research Trends in Rwanda — time-based analysis of topics.
- Publication Growth — how student research output evolves.
- University Rankings — contribution by institutions.
- Citation Analytics — tracking influence of articles/authors.
- Download Statistics — readership metrics.
- Interactive Maps — geospatial visualization.
- AI Trend Prediction — forecast emerging topics.

How to read this project

1. Start with the `01-top-diseases-published.md` and `02-research-trends-in-rwanda.md` files to understand the key metrics.
2. Use the `03-publication-growth.md` and `04-university-rankings.md` files for trend and ranking methodology.
3. For implementation, follow `07-interactive-maps.md` (maps) and `08-ai-trend-prediction.md` (trend prediction).
