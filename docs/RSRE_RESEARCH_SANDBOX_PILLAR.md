# RSRE Research Sandbox

The Research Sandbox is a protected experimental workspace for research practice, safe analysis and reproducibility.

## Boundary
- Public and synthetic datasets are first-class inputs.
- Authorized research data can be registered with an authorization reference.
- Direct identifiers are explicitly flagged.
- The Sandbox does not act as a patient-record repository.
- The Sandbox does not alter the RSJH editorial workflow.

## Workflow
Workspace → Dataset registration → Notes/analysis → Reproducibility → Promote a mature idea to the Research Incubator.

Promotion to the Incubator is additive and does not automatically create or publish an RSJH manuscript.
