# V29 Research Analytics UX

## Purpose
Turn Research Analytics into a decision-support surface rather than a dashboard of disconnected charts.

## Experience
Overview | Topics & diseases | Institutions | Trends

The overview surfaces interpretable evidence signals and routes users back to Research Discovery. Topic signals are explicitly framed as starting points for exploration, not as proof of a research gap.

## Boundaries
- Analytics interprets available published-record signals.
- Discovery searches evidence and sources.
- Sandbox is for experimentation/analysis.
- Incubator is for real study execution.
- Analytics does not rank universities, determine research quality, or make clinical/policy decisions.

## Backend
No new backend contract was required for V29. Existing analytics endpoints remain the source of truth.
