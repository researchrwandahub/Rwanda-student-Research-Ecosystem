# RSRE Support Model

## Non-negotiable principle
RSJH (Rwanda Student Journal for Health) is free. Students and researchers are not charged to submit, peer review, or publish. Sponsorship never buys acceptance, reviewer assignment, editorial preference, or publication.

## Public placement
The primary sponsorship CTA is **Support RSRE** on the homepage and footer, with a subtle header CTA on large screens. It is intentionally not presented as a student payment wall.

## Sponsor flow
Support RSRE → choose ecosystem support or research gift → payment/arrangement is handled sponsor-side → payment is confirmed by the configured payment process → beneficiary may receive a gift code by email → beneficiary redeems without paying.

## Gift scope
Gift sponsorship supports research-development/access purposes. Legacy database values that mention journal/publication support must not be surfaced as a payment-for-publication product.
