# RSRE MedTech AI Pillar

MedTech AI is a cross-pillar assistant, not a replacement for the RSRE pillars.

## Separation of roles
- Academy: learning, guided explanations, practical labs, validated questions and feedback.
- Discovery: published-research discovery and evidence-search assistance.
- Sandbox: experimentation, analysis and reproducibility support.
- Incubator: real project planning and execution support.
- Passport: evidence and progression record.
- RSJH: protected editorial and publication system.

## Safety boundaries
MedTech AI cannot grant ethics approval, accept/reject a manuscript, assign publication priority, diagnose/treat a patient, or claim citations it was not given.

## Evidence
The chat endpoint may pass only published RSJH records as research evidence. Private manuscripts are not exposed through the general AI chat.

## Provider model
The current provider abstraction supports mock mode for development and OpenAI-compatible providers when configured. This keeps the application functional even when no paid API credits are available.
