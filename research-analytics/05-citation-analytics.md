# Citation Analytics

Overview

Track citation counts per article and author to surface influential student research.

Key metrics

- Citations per article
- H-index per author (simple calculation from article citation counts)
- Top-cited articles

Data sources

- Manual entry or backend field for citations
- Google Scholar integration in future phases

Visuals

- Leaderboard of top-cited authors
- Time-series of citation accumulation for selected articles

Notes

Citation data is optional for Phase 1; collect when authors provide citation counts or integrate with external services later.
