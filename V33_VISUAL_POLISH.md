# V33 Visual Polish

## Product design system
- Unified RSRE visual tokens for ink, surfaces, borders, brand green, research blue, violet and amber accents.
- Refined shadows and rounded-card hierarchy so feature cards feel less generic.
- Stronger keyboard focus visibility and accessible form states.
- Consistent badges, KPI labels, metadata, dividers, empty states and skeleton loaders.
- Premium button hierarchy with primary, secondary and ghost actions.
- Subtle hero grid/orb helpers for flagship landing sections.
- Consistent scrollbar treatment for dense research workspaces.
- Responsive radius, KPI and control sizing for small screens.
- Preserves V32 reduced-motion support.

## Design principles
1. Trustworthy academic product, not a social-media aesthetic.
2. Motion is supportive; hierarchy does most of the communication.
3. RSJH remains visually distinct but part of the RSRE ecosystem.
4. Green is the primary action signal; blue is research/discovery; violet/amber are contextual accents.
5. Forms should feel calm and obvious, with strong focus states.
