
## 17. External product-quality reference review

Two external references were reviewed during the 2026-09-09 product pass.

### IBM SkillsBuild

IBM SkillsBuild publicly presents learning experiences with stated durations such as 60–90 minutes, progress tracking, learning pathways and digital credentials; IBM also describes practical/project-based learning and assessment requirements for credentials. This is useful as a maturity reference for RSRE Academy. It is not a template to copy. RSRE should apply the product principles—substantive learning, visible progression, practical application and evidence-backed credentials—while maintaining a distinct RSRE visual identity and research curriculum.

### KiraMedWell

The public KiraMedWell homepage presents a unified wellness product containing assessments, AI assistance, therapist booking, games, courses/certification and articles/videos, plus several numerical marketing claims. For RSRE, the useful lesson is product coherence and clear capability grouping. RSRE must not copy its design or claims. In particular, RSRE should only publish user counts, satisfaction figures, institutional relationships, credentials/accreditation or similar trust claims when those claims are actually substantiated.

## 18. Product-design conclusion

The central Academy issue is not that learners need to be prevented from finishing quickly. The problem is that some lesson records contain too little instructional substance for the estimated learning time. The correct fix is richer curriculum structure, meaningful practical work, assessment quality and honest metadata.
