# RSRE Ethics & Compliance — Pillar 10

## Purpose
Provide research-integrity, privacy, governance and ethics-readiness guidance across RSRE without pretending that RSRE itself grants statutory ethics approval.

## Boundaries
- Academy: teaches ethics and provides practical exercises.
- Sandbox: flags dataset/privacy risks before experimentation.
- Incubator: uses assessments to prepare projects for institutional review.
- RSJH: retains its existing publication ethics and editorial decision workflow.
- MedTech AI: provides responsible-use guidance but cannot make ethics or regulatory decisions.

## Readiness assessment
A researcher can create an assessment for a project, sandbox workspace, Academy practical lab or other activity. The engine identifies common risk signals including human participants, vulnerable groups, identifiable/sensitive data, biological samples/interventions and AI-assisted decision support. It returns guidance and a low/moderate/high internal risk signal.

## Important limitation
The assessment is a readiness aid. It does not certify that a study is exempt, approved, lawful or regulatorily authorized. Researchers must use the competent institutional/ethical/regulatory pathway applicable to their work.

## Momentum
Creating an assessment triggers an RSRE notification so the researcher knows the next action is available.
